## Defect D-1 (code, D-8, not fixed: stop and report)

`internal/plugins/rsvpte/fsm.go` `expiredPSBs` reads `PSB.LastRefresh` only. Nothing reads `resvStateBlock.LastRefresh`, so reservation state from downstream never times out. RFC 2205 §2.3 says: "The state is deleted if no matching refresh messages arrive before the expiration of a "cleanup timeout" interval."
Failing untagged test: `TestRFC2205ResvStateDeletedAtCleanupTimeout` in softstate_rfc2205_test.go. It is RED now: RSB kept, and LSP State Up.
The fix needs a design choice, which is why it is not made here: what an ingress or transit does when its RSB times out while its PSB lives. Options are to remove the reservation and send a ResvTear or a ResvErr upstream, to bring the LSP down and re-signal, or both. Recommendation: extend `cleanupTick` to expire RSBs on non-egress LSPs through `removeReservation`, and quote RFC 2205 §3.7 above it. No journal row was written, because this blocks RFC2205-2.3-1 (D-8).
