| 2026-08-31 | rfcgate-6-supported-extraction-signoff | `internal/component/bgp/plugins/bmp` -- `primeSender` and `BMPPlugin.bounceMonitoredPeers` vs `senderSession.writeRouteMonitoring` | A BMP collector is told a peer is up and is never given that peer's routes.
