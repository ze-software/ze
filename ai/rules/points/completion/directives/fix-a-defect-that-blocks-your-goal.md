**When a defect blocks a goal the current work exists to achieve, you FIX the defect.** You do not park it, move it to `tmp/`, file it as a deferral, or offer to drop the deliverable.
**This point covers the BLOCKING defect only, and the next one covers every other defect you find.** A defect the current goal does not depend on is separable: it gets one row in `plan/journal/<class>.md`, the work in hand closes, and nothing else is owed.
