|------|---------|
| `# ze point: <rule>/<slug>` | This check enforces that point. Several lines bind several points |
| `# ze point: none -- <why>` | This check enforces no written point, and the reason is REQUIRED. Without it, "nobody bound this yet" and "there is nothing to bind" look the same |
