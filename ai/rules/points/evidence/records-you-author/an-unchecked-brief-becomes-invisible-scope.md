**Every constraint you put in a delegate's brief MUST be traced to source first.** A delegate cannot audit your reasoning: it treats the brief as given, so an unverified constraint becomes its scope silently, and the work it was told not to do appears nowhere in its report.
**This standard is stricter than the one for your own reasoning**, because you can revise yours mid-task and it cannot.
