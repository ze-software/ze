**A claim about design INTENT, or about a foreign system, has a producer too, and it is not the nearest text.** A comment states what its author believed. The decisions actually taken are recorded in `plan/deferrals/`, `plan/journal/`, and specs. A generated binding stub states that a field exists, never what the system it binds to does with it.
**You MUST read the decision record before asserting intent, especially before calling something a mistake, and you MUST read a foreign system's own source before asserting its semantics.**
