**The instance a document NAMES is the one its author already looked at, so it is the member most likely to satisfy the claim and the least informative to check.** Confirming it feels like verification and carries almost no information.
**You MUST derive the set the claim covers, then count how many members hold.** The answer is a ratio, and a ratio short of the whole set changes what the work is.
