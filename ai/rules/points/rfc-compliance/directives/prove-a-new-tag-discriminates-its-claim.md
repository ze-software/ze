**A tag you ADD, and a tagged unit whose behavior or claim you CHANGE, MUST carry a discrimination record in the same change.** A tag's prose states what its test demonstrates, and no gate can read a sentence, so the proof is a recorded break of the producing code under which that tagged unit itself goes RED. `./le rfc discriminate stem <stem> report <path>` proposes the candidate breaks, `./le rfc discriminate-record` observes one and writes the record, and `./le rfc check` refuses a tagged unit the commit under test added against `HEAD^` and that carries none. The two proof routes, the closed escape vocabulary and the eleven refusals are in `docs/contributing/rfc-conformance-gates.md`.
**The claim MUST state what the test body checks, and MUST NOT state more.** A claim wider than the assertion converts an unproven MUST into a proven one on the public ledger, which is the violation with a green bar on top. Rewording a claim stales its record and owes a fresh one, because a proof of the old sentence is not a proof of the new one.
