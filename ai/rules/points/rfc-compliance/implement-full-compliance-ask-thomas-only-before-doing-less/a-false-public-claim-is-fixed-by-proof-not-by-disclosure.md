**A false public support claim MUST be corrected immediately on the surface that carries it to the status the evidence supports.** Reports MUST distinguish implemented-and-tested, implemented-but-unverified and unimplemented capabilities, and name the behavior exercised and any missing proof. Correcting the claim MUST NOT wait for new tests or absent-feature development; verification and defect fixes remain part of the agreed baseline work.
