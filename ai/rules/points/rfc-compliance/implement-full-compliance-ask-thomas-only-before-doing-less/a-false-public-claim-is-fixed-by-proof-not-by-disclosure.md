**A public row that over-claims MUST be corrected by PROVING what it says, and the downgrade MUST NOT be offered beside that (owner directive, 2026-09-05).** `docs/features/rfc-status.md` publishing `Supported` over untested MUSTs has two repairs: write the tagged tests, or lower the row. Only the first is a repair. Lowering it makes the LEDGER honest and leaves Ze exactly as non-conformant as it was, which is the definition of doing less, and the previous directive already bans placing it beside full compliance for Thomas to choose between.

**This one is banned specifically because it does not FEEL like doing less.** A gap, a deferral and a `{not-applicable}` announce themselves; correcting a false claim announces itself as integrity work, so it reaches the owner dressed as the honest option and is picked for that reason. Measured on 2026-09-05: RFC 5282 published `Supported` on 1 both-polarity proof out of 19 and RFC 3748 on 33 of 61, and the session put "write the 36 tests, or correct the two rows to `Partial`" to Thomas. He answered "the 36 missing tagged tests get written", which is what this rule already required and what he MUST NOT have been asked.

**The tell is a sentence offering the owner a choice whose two arms leave DIFFERENT amounts of the RFC proven.** Where one arm proves more, that arm is the answer and the other MUST NOT be written down. A choice between two ways of proving the same requirement is legitimate and stays open; a choice between proving a requirement and describing it accurately is not a choice.
