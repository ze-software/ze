**Internet-Drafts (`draft-*`) MUST NOT count in RFC conformance figures, RFC targets, or release-profile obligations, and RFC conformance MUST be counted separately from draft conformance.** An Internet-Draft is not a published RFC and carries no formal RFC requirement. Ze can implement, summarize, and test a draft feature and report draft conformance in its own separate count, while keeping `draft-*` documents out of RFC conformance counts and release targets until they are published as RFCs.
