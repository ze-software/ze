## Description

Ze sends IPv6 Router Advertisements on a LAN interface unit (RFC 4861). This is the job radvd does on other systems. Hosts build addresses by stateless address autoconfiguration (SLAAC), learn a default router, and learn DNS resolvers (RFC 8106 RDNSS, up to 8 servers). The `router-advertisement` container sits in the per-unit `ipv6` container. It carries the M and O flags, the per-prefix L and A flags, four timers, a prefix list, and the resolver block. The container is Linux only and netlink only. A `backend vpp` tree rejects it at config verify. Config verify applies the cross-leaf rules a YANG range cannot express. `minimum-interval` is at most 0.75 x `maximum-interval`, and `preferred-lifetime` is at most `valid-lifetime`. A prefix with host bits is rejected rather than masked, and so is the link-local prefix. Two zero values are legal input. `router-lifetime 0` advertises prefixes and resolvers while Ze is not a default router. `rdnss lifetime 0` tells hosts to stop using the resolvers. The `iface-ra` plugin owns the socket and the timers. Every advertisement leaves with Hop Limit 255. The sender joins `ff02::2` for Router Solicitations and sends to `ff02::1`. Each unsolicited interval is random between the two configured bounds, and a solicited answer is rate limited. A sender that stops sends one advertisement with a Router Lifetime of 0. Counters are `ze_iface_ra_sent_total{interface}` and `ze_iface_ra_solicited_total{interface}`. `ze doctor` reports `doctor-iface-ra-forwarding` when an advertising interface has IPv6 forwarding off, on the device or in the kernel's global switch. <!-- source: internal/component/iface/yang/ze-iface-conf.yang -- container router-advertisement --> <!-- source: internal/component/iface/config_ra.go -- raValidate, raParsePrefixEntry --> <!-- source: internal/plugins/iface/ra/sender_linux.go -- openRASocket, run, sendFinal --> <!-- source: internal/plugins/iface/ra/ifacera.go -- SetMetricsRegistry --> <!-- source: internal/core/ndp/schedule.go -- UnsolicitedInterval --> <!-- source: internal/plugins/iface/ra/doctor.go -- checkRAForwarding -->

A unit that names a WAN with `ipv6 delegated-prefix <wan>` is in the CE role (RFC 7084). Each advertisement it sends carries the unit's /64 from the delegation in a Prefix Information option with the L and A flags, and one Route Information option (RFC 4191) for the whole delegated prefix. Both carry the lifetimes that remain of the delegation at the moment of the send, so they never outlive it (RFC 9915 Section 6.3). A /64 whose delegation ended, or that the unit stopped numbering from, is advertised once with zero lifetimes and then dropped. The Router Lifetime follows two inputs: W, whether the main table holds an IPv6 default route through a WAN device the unit names, and P, whether the unit advertises a prefix (an operator `prefix` entry, or a delegated /64 still valid). With W and P both true it is the configured `router-lifetime`; otherwise it is 0, and the PIO and RIO are still sent while the delegation holds (RFC 7084 G-4, L-4, L-3). When the Router Lifetime crosses zero in either direction, or a /64 is retired, the next advertisement leaves at once instead of at the next periodic interval, within the 3 second spacing RFC 4861 Section 6.2.6 sets (RFC 7084 G-5). A unit with no `delegated-prefix` keeps the configured Router Lifetime whatever the WAN does. A WAN name Ze cannot resolve counts as holding no default route, logged once at WARN when it starts failing and once at INFO when it resolves. The `pppoe-dhcpv6-pd-ze-client` topology scenario proves the RIO route on a dhcpcd host, the configured Router Lifetime while the session is up, and Router Lifetime 0 once the access concentrator ends the session. <!-- source: internal/plugins/iface/ra/advertiser.go -- build, withRoute, routerLifetime, urgent --> <!-- source: internal/plugins/iface/ra/sender_linux.go -- run, wanResolver, holdsDefault --> <!-- source: internal/component/iface/reconcile_ra.go -- reconcileRA --> <!-- source: internal/core/ndp/ra.go -- writeRouteInformation --> <!-- source: internal/core/ndp/schedule.go -- CeaseWait --> <!-- source: internal/le/interoplab/topology/check_pppoe_dhcpv6_pd_lan.go -- checkDelegatedLAN -->
