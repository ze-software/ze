## Overview

Specifies how a BGP speaker retains routes across a NOTIFICATION message or a hold-timer expiry, rather than only across the session resets RFC 4724 covers. It adds the N bit to the Graceful Restart capability and the Hard Reset Cease subcode that says a peer must NOT retain routes.
