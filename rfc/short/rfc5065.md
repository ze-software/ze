## Overview

Specifies BGP confederations: an AS is divided into member-ASes that speak eBGP to each other, with AS_CONFED_SEQUENCE and AS_CONFED_SET path segments that are stripped before the path leaves the confederation. Ze implements route reflection (RFC 4456) as its iBGP scaling mechanism and does not implement confederations.
