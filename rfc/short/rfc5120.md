## Overview

Specifies Multi-Topology routing for IS-IS: independent SPF topologies carried in MT-capable TLVs so IPv4 and IPv6 can follow different paths through one IS-IS domain. Ze runs IS-IS single-topology dual-stack, where one SPF serves both address families.
