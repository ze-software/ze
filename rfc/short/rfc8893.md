**Purpose:** Requires a BGP speaker that does RPKI origin validation to offer it in egress and redistribution policy as well as ingress, judged on the effective origin AS the route carries as it will be sent.

**Scope:** BGP egress (eBGP export) and redistribution policy. No wire format; RFC 6811 semantics unchanged.

**Errata:** None affecting implementation.

## Terms (§1)

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Effective origin AS | The Route Origin ASN of the UPDATE to be sent to the neighbour, after configuration and outbound policy |

Changes that alter it: removal of private ASes, confederation handling (RFC 5065), AS migration (RFC 7705), AS path manipulation.

## Processing Order (§1, §3)

1. Apply the egress configuration and policy that can change the AS_PATH.
2. Compute the effective origin AS from the AS_PATH as it will be announced.
3. Apply origin validation (RFC 6811 Section 2, RFC 8481 Section 4) against that origin.
4. Decide on the validation state with the same policy primitives the ingress side offers.
