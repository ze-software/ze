**Purpose:** Adds two LCP codes, Identification (12) and Time-Remaining (13),
and four LCP configuration options: FCS-Alternatives (9),
Self-Describing-Padding (10), Callback (13) and Compound-Frames (15).

## LCP codes (Section 1)

| Code | Name | Fields after the LCP header |
|------|------|-----------------------------|
| 12 | Identification | Magic-Number (4), Message (text) |
| 13 | Time-Remaining | Magic-Number (4), Seconds-Remaining (4), Message (text) |

The Magic-Number is zero until the Magic-Number option is negotiated, and
the Identifier changes on every packet sent.
