## Overview

Specifies Virtual Private LAN Service with LDP as the signalling plane: the PWid and Generalized PWid FEC elements that set up pseudowires between VPLS PEs, MAC address withdrawal, and the split-horizon rule for a full mesh of PWs. Ze implements the competing BGP-signalled plane of RFC 4761 instead, and the two are alternative signalling planes for the same service.
