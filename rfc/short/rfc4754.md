**Purpose:** Defines three IKE and IKEv2 authentication methods, ECDSA-256, ECDSA-384 and ECDSA-521, each binding an elliptic curve group to a hash function, and the fixed-length r-and-s signature encoding carried in the AUTH payload.

**Ze context:** `internal/component/ike/wire/payload_auth.go` declares methods 1 (RSA), 2 (PSK), 3 (DSS) and 14 (Digital Signature). `internal/component/ike/engine/auth.go::verifyRemoteAuth` dispatches on 2, 14 and 1 and answers "unsupported remote auth method" for anything else. ECDSA P-256 and P-384 keys sign and verify only inside method 14 (RFC 7427), where the signature is ASN.1 DER, not the concatenation this RFC defines.

## Methods (Sections 4 and 8)

| Method | IKEv2 Auth Method | Curve group | Hash | r, s bits | Signature bits |
|--------|-------------------|-------------|------|-----------|----------------|
| ECDSA-256 | 9 | 256-bit random ECP (group 19) | SHA-256 | 256 | 512 |
| ECDSA-384 | 10 | 384-bit random ECP (group 20) | SHA-384 | 384 | 768 |
| ECDSA-521 | 11 | 521-bit random ECP (group 21) | SHA-512 | 528 | 1056 |

## Encoding Rules (Section 7)

- Signature payload = r || s, each a big-endian integer of the fixed bit length above.
- Short values are left-padded with zeros to the fixed length.
- r and s are defined by the test-vector notation of Section 8.

## Pitfalls

- An implementation that already supports RFC 7427 must not reuse its DER signature for methods 9 to 11: the encodings differ.
- ECDSA-521 pads r and s to 528 bits (66 octets), not 521.
