**Purpose:** Adds six Acct-Status-Type values (Tunnel-Start 9, Tunnel-Stop 10,
Tunnel-Reject 11, Tunnel-Link-Start 12, Tunnel-Link-Stop 13,
Tunnel-Link-Reject 14) and two attributes, Acct-Tunnel-Connection (68) and
Acct-Tunnel-Packets-Lost (86), so a tunnel endpoint can account for tunnels
and the links inside them.
