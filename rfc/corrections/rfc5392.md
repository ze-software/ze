# RFC 5392 corrections

Why a requirement row of `rfc/short/rfc5392.md` changed level, text, or citation,
or was retired. The summary is the working document; this file is the record the
ratchets read (`checkLevelRatchet`, `checkRetiredRequirements`,
`internal/le/rfc/check_ratchets.go`).

Correction 2026-09-30: `RFC5392-3.2.1-4` quoted the whole §3.2.1 sentence, "The
Remote-AS-Number sub-TLV MUST be included in the Link TLV of both the Inter-AS-TE-v2
LSA and Inter-AS-TE-v3 LSA.", which carries two clauses. Ze meets the Inter-AS-TE-v2
clause (sub-TLV 21 in every originated type-6 Link TLV, and a received one without it
is skipped) and implements no Inter-AS-TE-v3 LSA (function code 13). Under main-thread
ruling R3 the row now quotes the span that ends at the v2 LSA, "The Remote-AS-Number
sub-TLV MUST be included in the Link TLV of both the Inter-AS-TE-v2 LSA", and keeps its
id, its [MUST] level and its tests. The v3 clause is the new row `RFC5392-3.2.1-5`,
which quotes the whole sentence under a `{gap}` naming only the v3 clause, because the
v3 half is not a self-standing span of the sentence. Extraction site 3.2.1:2 stays
mapped to `RFC5392-3.2.1-4`; `RFC5392-3.2.1-5` is listed under the section's
`unsourced-ids`, as `RFC5392-3.2.1-2` is for the parallel Neighbor ID clause.
