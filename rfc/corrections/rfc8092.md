Keep the existing reverse unsourced-ids entry, with no automatic exclusion or claim of implemented aggregation. No existing audit entry was found; later aggregate-union judgment would be FIRST. LargeCommunity codecs do not prove aggregation. Applicability/behavior remains unproven, not automatically not-applicable.
