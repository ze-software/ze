# RFC 2661 correction records

Correction 2026-09-28: `RFC2661-7.1-1` is a new row. The narrowing audit found that `RFC2661-6.1-1`, `RFC2661-6.2-1` and `RFC2661-24.10-1` each drop the §7.1 consequence of a message that breaks them: "Receipt of an invalid or unrecoverable malformed control message should be logged appropriately and the control connection cleared to ensure recovery to a known state." The keyword is lowercase, so the row is SHOULD. It carries no extractor site, so the §7.1 section entry of `rfc/extraction/rfc2661.json` lists it under `unsourced-ids`.

Correction 2026-09-28: `RFC2661-6.1-1` keeps its text. Its dropped obligation, clearing the control connection when an SCCRQ is "missing a required AVP", is the §7.1 sentence that `RFC2661-7.1-1` now rows.

Correction 2026-09-28: `RFC2661-24.10-1` keeps its text. Its dropped obligation, refusing a peer's zero Tunnel or Session ID with a StopCCN, is the §7.1 case of a message that "contains an AVP that is formatted incorrectly or whose value is out of range", which `RFC2661-7.1-1` now rows. Its text is the text of `RFC2661-10-2`, both quoting §5.3; one of the two is a retirement candidate under D-2.

Correction 2026-09-28: `RFC2661-6.2-1` keeps its text and gets no split row. Its dropped obligation is a §7.2.1 state-table transition, "wait-ctl-reply Receive SCCRP, Send StopCCN, idle not acceptable Clean up", with no sentence and no keyword. An SCCRP missing a required AVP is malformed, so the clearing it states is the §7.1 sentence `RFC2661-7.1-1` rows.

Correction 2026-09-28: `RFC2661-10-1` keeps its text and gets no split row. The narrowing audit named "a CDN is valid in any non-idle session state", which §7.4.1, §7.4.2, §7.5.1 and §7.5.2 state only as state-table transitions such as "established Receive CDN Clean up idle", with no sentence and no keyword. Its MUST sentence is also the text of `RFC2661-6.12-1`.

Correction 2026-09-28: `RFC2661-5.8-3` keeps its text. Its parenthetical, "(a recommended default is 5, but SHOULD be configurable)", is the obligation `RFC2661-5.8-8` rows at SHOULD, so the configurability clause is judged there and `RFC2661-5.8-3` carries the MUST that the tunnel and all sessions within are cleared.
