# RFC 4552 corrections

Correction 2026-09-30: `RFC4552-11-3` quoted the whole §11 rule table for interfaces with
authentication or confidentiality enabled, rules 2 to 5. Rules 4 and 5, "src/128 dst/128
OSPF protect" and "src/128 dst/128 ESP/OSPF or AH/OSPF protect", secure virtual-link
packets, and §11 places their installation on the transit-area interfaces in the next
sentence, which is the gap row `RFC4552-11-4`: Ze implements no virtual-link IPsec. The row
now quotes the span through rule 3, the rules Ze installs, and keeps its level and tests.
