# RFC 5709 corrections

Why a requirement row of `rfc/short/rfc5709.md` changed level, text, or citation, or
was retired. The summary is the working document; this file is the record the
ratchets read (`checkLevelRatchet`, `checkRetiredRequirements`,
`internal/le/rfc/check_ratchets.go`).

Correction 2026-09-30: `RFC5709-3.2-2` quoted the whole §3.2 sentence, "In the event that
the last key associated with an interface expires, it is unacceptable to revert to an
unauthenticated condition, and not advisable to disrupt routing.", as one MUST NOT row.
The sentence carries two obligations of different strength, and the second one is stated
by the next sentence: "Therefore, the router should send a "last Authentication Key
expiration" notification to the network manager and treat the key as having an infinite
lifetime until the lifetime is extended, the key is deleted by network management, or a
new key is configured." Under ruling R35 the row now quotes the span through "revert to an
unauthenticated condition" and keeps its level and its tests (the signer keeps AuType 2,
the verifier refuses AuType 0). The notification and the infinite lifetime are
`RFC5709-3.2-5`, a SHOULD row. Ze met neither before this change: its verifier refused
every packet signed with an expired last key, so two Ze routers whose last key expired
lost their adjacency. Both directions now keep the last key and notify once.
