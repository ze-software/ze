# RFC 3032 corrections

Why a requirement row of `rfc/short/rfc3032.md` changed level, text, or citation,
or was retired. The summary is the working document; this file is the record the
ratchets read (`checkLevelRatchet`, `checkRetiredRequirements`,
`internal/le/rfc/check_ratchets.go`).

Correction 2026-09-30: `RFC3032-2.1-1` quotes items iv and v of the §2.1 reserved
label list (Implicit NULL, and "Values 4-15 are reserved."), and items i to iii
(labels 0, 1 and 2) get no row of their own. Read §2.1, §2.2 and RFC 4182 §1 to §3.
Items i and iii stated that IPv4 and IPv6 Explicit NULL are "only legal at the bottom
of the label stack". RFC 4182, which updates RFC 3032, removes that restriction: it
changes "the unnecessary restriction, so that the two aforementioned label values are
legal anywhere in the label stack", so the placement rule binds nobody. What remains
of items i and iii is the disposition, "It indicates that the label stack must be
popped, and the forwarding of the packet must then be based on the IPv4 header.",
which `rfc/extraction/rfc3032.json` sites 2.1:1 and 2.1:2 already exclude as
binds-another-role: popping and forwarding a labeled packet is the kernel AF_MPLS or
VPP datapath, not Ze. Item ii, the Router Alert label, states where label 1 may sit
("legal anywhere in the label stack except at the bottom") and how a receiver
processes it; the processing is the same datapath role, and the placement rule is
conditional on a stack carrying label 1, which Ze never builds. No Ze label
allocator hands out label 1: RSVP-TE starts at firstDynamicLabel, 1000
(`rsvpte/fsm.go`, TestLSPTableAllocateSkipsReservedLabels, already on this row),
the LDP LIB allocates from 16 and wraps back to 16 (`ldp/lib.go`
allocateLabelLocked), and the OSPF SRGB and SRLB and the BGP EPE ranges are
configured from 16 (their YANG ranges start at 16). The reserved values Ze does use
are Explicit NULL (0, 2) and Implicit NULL (3), for the penultimate hop. A BGP
labeled route carries whatever label the operator configures (0 to 1048575), but
Ze only advertises that label in the NLRI and programs no MPLS push for it. Ze
programs no Router Alert label. Corrected 2026-09-30: an earlier wording said "Ze
assigns no label below firstDynamicLabel", which holds only for RSVP-TE. None of the three items adds an obligation
Ze fills, so no row is added and the row keeps its id, text and level.
