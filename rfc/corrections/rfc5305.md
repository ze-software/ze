# RFC 5305 corrections

Why a requirement row of `rfc/short/rfc5305.md` changed level, text, or citation.
The summary is the working document; this file is the record the level
ratchet reads (`checkLevelRatchet`, `internal/le/rfc/check_ratchets.go`).

Correction 2026-09-28: `RFC5305-4.1-1` quoted two obligations of Section 4.1 as one
row. Its last sentence, "If a prefix is advertised from one area to another at the
same level, then the up/down bit SHALL be set to 1.", binds a same-level inter-area
advertisement, which is a different condition from the level-to-level leak the rest
of the row governs. The sentence is now its own row, `RFC5305-4.1-3`, and
`RFC5305-4.1-1` ends at "there are no persistent forwarding loops". The extraction
site 4.1:3 moves to the new row.

Retired 2026-09-30: `RFC5305-3-2` binds only a traffic engineering SPF
implementation, a feature Ze does not provide. Read §3, §3.7 and §4: the sentence
is "To preclude overflow within a traffic engineering Shortest Path First (SPF)
implementation, all metrics greater than or equal to MAX_PATH_METRIC SHALL be
considered to have a metric of MAX_PATH_METRIC.", and §3.7 repeats it for the TE
Default metric sub-TLV. Ze computes no TE path: owner decision 2026-09-21 ("Keep
configured routes"), the scope decision `RFC5305-3.7-1` already records, so the
obligation is conditional on an absent feature. Its extraction sites 3:1 and
3.7:1 are excluded as `feature-out-of-scope`. Its two tags on TestISISMetricWidth
in internal/plugins/isis/spf/spf_test.go were deleted: that unit proves the
normal-SPF path clamp, which RFC 5308 §5 obliges and `RFC5308-5-2`, tagged on the
same unit, already claims.
