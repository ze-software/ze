# RFC 1994 corrections

Why a requirement row of `rfc/short/rfc1994.md` changed level, text, or citation.

Retired 2026-09-28: `RFC1994-1.1-1` states no obligation RFC 1994 carries. The row
quoted "An implementation which does not include this option MUST be prepared to
interoperate with another implementation which does include the option." Read §1.1:
the sentence is the second half of the definition of MAY in the keyword glossary,
and "this option" names any item a MAY makes optional, not the LCP
Authentication-Protocol option. No CHAP behaviour follows from it, and the extraction
site now excludes it as glossary text beside the MUST NOT definition. Its two tags, on
TestNegotiatePeerAuthProtoRejected and TestNegotiatePeerAuthProtoAccepted in
internal/component/l2tp/ppp/lcp_options_test.go, claimed only this row and were
deleted; the tests stay.

Correction 2026-09-30: `RFC1994-4.2-2` quotes the §4.2 Failure sentence only up to
"set to 4 (Failure)", and the narrowing audit of 2026-09-28 found its SHOULD clause
dropped: "and SHOULD take action to terminate the link." The clause is now its own row,
`RFC1994-4.2-5` [SHOULD], quoting the whole sentence, with the MUST clause left on
`RFC1994-4.2-2`. It states for §4.2 what `RFC1994-2-1` states in the §2 overview,
"otherwise the connection SHOULD be terminated", and both rows are tagged on
TestRFC1994CHAPFailureTerminatesTheLink (positive: a rejected Response draws a Failure,
then an EventSessionDown that makes the transport take the link down) and
TestRFC1994CHAPSuccessKeepsTheLink (negative: an accepted Response draws a Success and
no EventSessionDown), in internal/component/l2tp/ppp/rfc1994_failure_terminates_link_test.go.
