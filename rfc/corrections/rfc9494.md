# rfc9494 correction records

Correction 2026-10-06: `RFC9494-4.2-1` retains its id and quote. The immediately following sentence in §4.2 states: "The interval for which they are retained is limited by the sum of the Restart Time in the received Graceful Restart Capability and the Long-Lived Stale Time in the received Long-Lived Graceful Restart Capability." This bounds the retention already carried by `RFC9494-4.2-1`; it is not permission to retain routes indefinitely. The existing tagged `TestRFC9494DelayedDownPreservesLLSTDeadlines` in `internal/component/bgp/plugins/gr/rfc9494_llgr_entry_test.go` exercises both JSON and structured DOWN paths against the registered RIB with delayed DOWN processing, zero and nonzero Restart Time, and distinct IPv4/IPv6 LLSTs. It asserts presence one nanosecond before each original combined deadline and absence at the deadline, or immediate removal when DOWN processing has already consumed it. `onSessionDownDeferred`, `startRestartTimer`, `handleTimerExpired` and `enterLLGRLocked` in `internal/component/bgp/plugins/gr/gr_state.go` retain the original DOWN/GR deadline and subtract elapsed time when arming each LLST timer. The existing timer and expiry rows `RFC9494-4.2-2` and `RFC9494-4.2-3` carry the associated timer actions. No second id is allocated for this already-tested bound, and no existing row, tag or audit verdict changes. This disposition does not close the separate consecutive-restart, synchronization or live-workflow obligations.
