# RFC 2759 corrections

Retired 2026-09-27: `RFC2759-x-11` states no obligation RFC 2759 carries. Read §1 to
§10, then searched the whole text for reject, discard, ignore, invalid and non-zero:
no sentence says a receiver must reject a Response whose Reserved or Flags octets
are not zero. §4 states only the sender-side format, "8 octets: Reserved, must be
zero" and "The Flag field is reserved for future use and MUST be zero.", which rows
RFC2759-x-1 and RFC2759-x-2 already carry. Its three tags, on the "valid response
accepted", "non-zero reserved rejected" and "non-zero flags rejected" subtests of
`TestMSCHAPv2ResponseFieldValidation` in `internal/core/eap/rfc2759_eap_mschapv2_test.go`,
were deleted rather than moved, because the RFC2759-x-1 and RFC2759-x-2 tags on the
same three subtests already make the same claims with the same polarity. It had no
discrimination record and no audit verdict.

Correction 2026-09-28: `RFC2759-x-8` and `RFC2759-x-9` move from MUST to SHOULD.
Read §3 and §4. The only BCP 14 keyword in either quoted span is "the standard
guidelines on randomness [1,2,7] SHOULD be observed". The rest of each span, "send
an 16-octet challenge Value field" and "is generated by the peer and is used in the
calculation of the NT-Response field", is format prose with no keyword, and D-3 keeps
a level over such prose only when no other keyword is present. The quotes are
unchanged, and the tags stay where they were.

Retired 2026-10-11: `RFC2759-x-10` is example data, not an obligation, by owner decision (Thomas, 2026-10-11: "x-3 MUST, retire x-10/x-12 (Recommended)"). Read §9.2, §8.3 and §4, then the whole text: the quoted line is the "0-to-256-unicode-char Password" octets of the §9.2 Hash Example, a worked vector for the password "clientPass", and no sentence of RFC 2759 makes those octets a requirement. The UTF-16LE encoding the vector pins is stated by the §8.3 NtPasswordHash() pseudocode, which no row carries. Its four tags, the positive and negative on `TestNtPasswordHash` in `internal/core/eap/rfc2759_mschapv2_test.go` and on `TestRFC2759PasswordHashVector` in `internal/core/eap/rfc2759_clause_test.go`, were deleted rather than moved, because no row states the obligation they prove. The tests stay as known-answer vectors, and their four discrimination records and the audit verdict were dropped with the row.

Retired 2026-10-11: `RFC2759-x-12` is example data, not an obligation, by owner decision (Thomas, 2026-10-11: "x-3 MUST, retire x-10/x-12 (Recommended)"). Read §9.1.3, §6 and §9.1, then the whole text: the quoted line is the message-flow diagram of the §9.1.3 example "Failed authentication with no retry allowed", and the §6 error-code table it draws on is indicative. No sentence of RFC 2759 obliges an authenticator to answer a failed NT-Response with E=691 R=0. Its four tags, on `TestRFC2759FailureInvitesNoRetry` in `internal/core/eap/rfc2759_clause_test.go` and on `TestRFC2759AuthenticatorRefusesWithErrorCode691` and `TestRFC2759AuthenticatorAcceptsMatchingNTResponse` in `internal/core/eap/rfc2759_failure_packet_test.go`, were deleted rather than moved, because no row states the obligation they prove. The tests stay as example-flow vectors, and their four discrimination records and the audit verdict were dropped with the row.
