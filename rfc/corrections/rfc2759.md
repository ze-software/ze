# RFC 2759 corrections

Retired 2026-09-27: `RFC2759-x-11` states no obligation RFC 2759 carries. Read §1 to
§10, then searched the whole text for reject, discard, ignore, invalid and non-zero:
no sentence says a receiver must reject a Response whose Reserved or Flags octets
are not zero. §4 states only the sender-side format, "8 octets: Reserved, must be
zero" and "The Flag field is reserved for future use and MUST be zero.", which rows
RFC2759-x-1 and RFC2759-x-2 already carry. Its three tags, on the "valid response
accepted", "non-zero reserved rejected" and "non-zero flags rejected" subtests of
`TestMSCHAPv2ResponseFieldValidation` in `internal/core/eap/eap_mschapv2_test.go`,
were deleted rather than moved, because the RFC2759-x-1 and RFC2759-x-2 tags on the
same three subtests already make the same claims with the same polarity. It had no
discrimination record and no audit verdict.

Correction 2026-09-28: `RFC2759-x-8` and `RFC2759-x-9` move from MUST to SHOULD.
Read §3 and §4. The only BCP 14 keyword in either quoted span is "the standard
guidelines on randomness [1,2,7] SHOULD be observed". The rest of each span, "send
an 16-octet challenge Value field" and "is generated by the peer and is used in the
calculation of the NT-Response field", is format prose with no keyword, and D-3 keeps
a level over such prose only when no other keyword is present. The quotes are
unchanged, and the tags stay where they were.
