Correction 2026-09-28: `RFC3786-5-1` quoted all of Section 5 as one row. The last
sentence, "If an LSP fragment 0 of an extended LSP set is missing or its
RemainingLifetime is zero, only that LSP set MUST NOT be considered in the SPF.",
binds an extended LSP set, which Ze does not originate or recognise. It is now its
own row, `RFC3786-5-2`, marked not-applicable under the 2026-09-21 owner decision
that keeps standard fragment sets. `RFC3786-5-1` keeps the Original LSP set
obligation and ends at "the large logical LSP is not considered in the SPF". The
dropped sentence on is-alias-id identification describes extended sets and carries
no obligation of its own. The extraction site 5:2 moves to the new row.
