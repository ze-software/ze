# draft-ietf-idr-linklocal-capability correction records

Correction 2026-10-01: `DRAFT-IETF-IDR-LINKLOCAL-CAPABILITY-4-15` is a new row, split from `DRAFT-IETF-IDR-LINKLOCAL-CAPABILITY-4-3` under main-thread rulings R3 and R57(a). §4 reads "If the route is directly connected to the speaker, or if the interface address of the router through which the announced network is reachable for the speaker is the internal peer's address, the next hop MUST include its own Link- Local IPv6 address." The sentence carries two conditions. The second one names a route whose next hop is the receiving internal peer's own address, and RFC 4271 Section 5.1.3 reads "A route originated by a BGP speaker SHALL NOT be advertised to a peer using an address of that peer as NEXT_HOP." Ze withholds such a route on every rail (owner decision 2026-08-15), so the second condition's antecedent never arises: the new row quotes that clause and is annotated `{not-applicable}`, naming the untagged test that shows nothing reaches the wire. The predicate branch that classified the internal peer's address for this condition was dead and is removed from `linkScope.linkLocalNextHop`. `DRAFT-IETF-IDR-LINKLOCAL-CAPABILITY-4-3` keeps the whole sentence as its quote and keeps its tests, which prove the first condition (a route reachable through the speaker carries the speaker's own Link-Local) in both polarities.

Correction 2026-10-01: `DRAFT-IETF-IDR-LINKLOCAL-CAPABILITY-1-2` is a new row (main-thread ruling R58(c)). The -06 text gives Section 1 three derived sites the extraction did not hold. Site 1:2 reads "Since IPv6 Link-Local addresses are not required to be globally unique, implementations must ensure that they are strictly associated with a specific interface." It binds an implementation, so it is a row of its own, quoted verbatim at level MUST, and annotated {gap}: Ze keeps no interface with a received Link-Local next hop (the RIB publishes it bare) and does not tie the configured `session link-local` address to the session's interface. Sites 1:1 and 1:3, and the Copyright Notice site front:1, are excluded with reasons in `rfc/extraction/draft-ietf-idr-linklocal-capability.json`.

Correction 2026-10-01: the Enrolment reason counted "twelve MUST-level requirements" and one outstanding `{gap}`. The checklist holds fifteen MUST-level rows after 4-15 and 1-2 were added, and two of them carry a `{gap}` (6-1 and 1-2); the cell now says so.

Correction 2026-10-02: the `{gap}` annotation of `DRAFT-IETF-IDR-LINKLOCAL-CAPABILITY-6-1` named the wrong half. It said the gap was that validateMPReachNextHop answers a next-hop length outside attribute.ValidNextHopLens with a session reset rather than treat-as-withdraw. §6 places "If the Next Hop field is malformed, the implementation MUST handle the malformed UPDATE message using the approach of "treat-as- withdraw", as described in section 7.3 of [RFC7606]." after the paragraphs about a receiver of "an MP_REACH_NLRI with the length of the Next Hop Field set to 32, where the update contains anything other than a Global IPv6 followed by a Link-Local IPv6 address", so "malformed" is the content of a field whose length is admitted. A wrong length stays under RFC 7606 Section 7.11: "Since the next hop precedes the NLRI field in the attribute, in this case it will not be possible to reliably locate the NLRI; thus, the "session reset" or "AFI/SAFI disable" approach MUST be used." Treat-as-withdraw needs the NLRI located, so the session reset Ze sends for a wrong length is correct. The annotation and the Support coverage cell now name only the real gap: on a capability-77 session no receive producer inspects the addresses inside an admitted 32-octet Next Hop field. The row's quote, level and verdict (unimplemented) are unchanged.
