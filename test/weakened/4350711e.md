# Test weakenings this commit accepts

Four entries, of three kinds. TWO are the same property asserted under a new
name, against the model instead of against a rendered page. ONE is a real
deletion: the stage it drove is gone, and a successor test asserts that absence.
The fourth is the assertion count that follows that deletion. Nothing about
surviving behavior stopped being asserted.

| Test | Reason |
|------|--------|
| TestTheDocumentIndexHoldsEveryCodePath | Deleted because it became a tautology, not because its claim stopped mattering. It compared `docstocode.DocumentsByPath(root)` against `loadDocumentIndex(root)`, and this spec reduced `loadDocumentIndex` to `return docstocode.DocumentsByPath(root)`, so both halves compared one function with itself and neither could fail. `TestTheAuditReportsEveryDocumentOfABulletRenderedPath` replaces it and asserts the thing that matters: `AuditAnchors` over the REAL tree reports the design document of a path in a bullet-rendered package. Its discrimination was proved by re-imposing the table-only blind spot in `loadDocumentIndex`, under which it names the document the audit missed. |
| anchors_test | The file's assertion count falls from 25 to 24. Both dropped assertions are the two halves of the tautology above; the replacement test adds its own. |
| TestSessionStartBuildsEveryRegisteredDerivedArtifact | Renamed and narrowed on purpose, and the narrowing is the fix this commit carries. It required the hook to build EVERY registered artifact, which is what put a 194-shard render inside a 5 second budget and took the hook to 5.32 seconds. `TestSessionStartBuildsEveryArtifactDeclaredForIt` asserts the same property over the artifacts declared `SessionStartBuild`, and `TestSessionStartRendersNoDeferredArtifact` asserts the other half, which nothing asserted before. The pair covers strictly more than the original: the original could not tell a hook that builds everything from one that builds the right set. |
