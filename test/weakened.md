| TestShowPKICertBadSubcommand | Replaced, and the ground it covered is now covered by more tests than before. It asserted that `handleShowPKICertificate` refused a bad tail while that handler owned a four-arm switch over `pem`, `bundle pem`, `fingerprint` and the bare detail form.
