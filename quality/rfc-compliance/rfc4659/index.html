Enrolment

Enrolled: BGP-MPLS IPv6 VPN / VPNv6 (RFC 4659): labeled VPNv6 NLRI, AFI2/SAFI128 capability negotiation, and configured zero-RD global-IPv6 next-hop emission. The widened originator-address obligation has independent recipient-wire proof. IPv4-mapped-IPv6 and global-plus-link-local next-hop gaps remain explicit; data-plane PE tunneling and the integrated multi-AS ASBR service remain outside the implemented baseline. Generic VPNv6 origination and cross-peer forwarding do not establish the option-(b) service.

What the public ledger says

Status: Partial

What the ledger says is covered VPNv6 NLRI (RD + MPLS label + IPv6 prefix) encode/decode, AFI=2/SAFI=128 capability negotiation, and the zero-RD + global-IPv6 24-octet next-hop. Native configured self and explicit IPv6 next-hop selection is checked on actual recipient Sessions under both grouping modes, against independently speaker-owned interface addresses. This is control-plane origination proof, not tunnel creation.
What the ledger says remains The IPv4-mapped-IPv6 VPN next hop is written for every IPv4 next hop of a VPN-IPv6 route, but Ze never evaluates the IPv4-tunneling condition and no tagged recipient-wire test proves it (RFC4659-3.2.1.2-1, RFC4659-8-4); RFC4659-8-3 is not credited by the 48-octet global+link-local next-hop and common-subnet decision now implemented for RFC4659-3.2.1.1-3. Data-plane PE tunneling (Section 4) and integrated multi-AS ASBR options (Section 8 a/b) are not performed. RFC4659-8-5 remains an explicit option-(b) implementation gap: generic VPNv6 IPv6 next-hop encoding and cross-peer forwarding are implemented, but the integrated inter-ASBR label-binding/forwarding path is not. The conditional SHALL is retained without whole-requirement credit; no ASBR feature is commissioned. The widened RFC4659-3.2.1.1-1 has been independently rejudged against configured recipient-wire proof; the link-local-only unspecified-global exception remains.
