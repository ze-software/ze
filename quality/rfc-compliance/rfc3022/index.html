Enrolment

Not enrolled (non-normative, the document imposes no MUST-level obligation on an implementation, so there is nothing to gate): Traditional IP Network Address Translator, published with IETF category Informational. The text carries no RFC 2119 key-words section and a capitalised MUST / MUST NOT / SHALL / SHALL NOT / REQUIRED / SHOULD / MAY scan over rfc/full/rfc3022.txt finds zero keywords. Its lowercase "must" sentences describe how the Basic NAT and NAPT operations it defines rewrite a packet; the normative NAT behavioral requirements are stated by RFC 4787 (UDP) and RFC 5382 (TCP), which carry their own summaries.
