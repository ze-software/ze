Enrolled: OSPF as the PE/CE Protocol for BGP/MPLS IP VPNs

What the public ledger says

Status: Not supported

What the ledger says is covered:

Ordinary OSPF: area and external routing, RFC 6549 instances, cryptographic authentication, backbone virtual links and plain unicast redistribution. These are not RFC 4577 VPN-PE import/export.
What the ledger says remains Native VPN-PE behavior is not selected: no OSPF-domain/VRF association, VPN metadata import/export, VPN Route Tag loop prevention or optional sham links. Ordinary routing and CE obligations remain mandatory and tested under their governing RFCs.
