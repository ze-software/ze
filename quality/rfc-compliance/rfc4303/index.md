## Enrolment

Enrolled: IP Encapsulating Security Payload (RFC 4303): 1 MET (SPI 0/reserved never on the wire) + 1 single-polarity positive (32-packet anti-replay window projected) + 14 not-applicable (per-packet ESP datapath -- sequence, padding, TFC, SAD lookup, ICV, anti-replay check -- kernel XFRM)

## What the public ledger says

**Status:** Partial

**What the ledger says is covered:**

ESP SA parameter model, protocol 50, tunnel and transport modes, XFRM installation, OSPFv3 manual ESP.

**What the ledger says remains**

Integrity-only ESP is configurable on OSPFv3 manual SAs ([`RFC4303-1-1`](#rfc4303-1-1)) but not negotiable in IKEv2, whose ESP vocabulary has no ENCR_NULL ([`RFC4303-1-2`](#rfc4303-1-2), gap). SPI 0 and anti-replay-needs-integrity are proven on one SA producer each ([`RFC4303-2.1-1`](#rfc4303-2.1-1), [`RFC4303-3.4.3-2`](#rfc4303-3.4.3-2)). Protocol 50 ([`RFC4303-2-1`](#rfc4303-2-1)) is proven on the wire for the OSPFv3 manual SAs and at the installed SA for the IKE Child SA; the address-match indication for the inbound SA lookup ([`RFC4303-2.1-2`](#rfc4303-2.1-2)) is proven against the kernel's own lookup on both producers.
