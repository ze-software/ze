# First-hop redundancy

Technology aggregate. First-hop redundancy. The RFCs whose summary names the fhrp aggregate, with their mandatory and optional coverage.

[All profiles](../) · [All RFCs](../../)

- **Mandatory**: 67.5% (112 of 166 (67.5%)). MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL and SHALL NOT rows of the walked RFCs that a recorded test break proves.
- **Optional**: 6.2% (4 of 65 (6.2%)). MAY, NOT RECOMMENDED, OPTIONAL, RECOMMENDED, SHOULD and SHOULD NOT rows, counted the same way.
- **Not walked**: 0 (of 3 RFCs). RFCs with no valid extraction sign-off: their rows count in neither figure.
- **Mandatory register**: not complete (every row proven, none unwalked). A profile is complete only when it requires at least one RFC and none of them is unwalked.
