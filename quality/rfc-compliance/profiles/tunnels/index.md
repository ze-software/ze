# Tunnels

Technology aggregate. IP-in-IP, GRE, and IPv6 transition tunnels. The RFCs whose summary names the tunnels aggregate, with their mandatory and optional coverage.

[All profiles](../) · [All RFCs](../../)

- **Mandatory**: 3.3% (2 of 61 (3.3%)). MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHALL and SHALL NOT rows of the walked RFCs that a recorded test break proves.
- **Optional**: 0.0% (0 of 64 (0.0%)). MAY, NOT RECOMMENDED, OPTIONAL, RECOMMENDED, SHOULD and SHOULD NOT rows, counted the same way.
- **Not walked**: 0 (of 5 RFCs). RFCs with no valid extraction sign-off: their rows count in neither figure.
- **Mandatory register**: not complete (every row proven, none unwalked). A profile is complete only when it requires at least one RFC and none of them is unwalked.
