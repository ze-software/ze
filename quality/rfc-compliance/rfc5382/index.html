Enrolment

Enrolled: NAT behavioral requirements for TCP (BCP 142). Every MUST-level row is classified: all 11 are {gap}: the behaviors neither ze nor the Linux defaults provide (endpoint-independent mapping, no port overloading, the unsolicited-SYN hold, the four-minute transitory timeout, a floor on an operator-set established timeout, hairpinning), and the three nf_conntrack performs on the nft firewall backend (RFC5382-4.2-1, RFC5382-4.2-2, RFC5382-7.3-1), which stay unverified because an operator may select the vpp backend, whose VPP NAT44-ED path (internal/plugins/firewall/vpp/nat_linux.go, applyMasquerade, applySNAT, applyDNAT) no test or source reading has checked.

What the public ledger says

Status: Partial

What the ledger says is covered
  • Source NAT, destination NAT and masquerade are configured through the firewall YANG tree and lowered into nftables NAT chains on the nft firewall backend, or programmed into VPP NAT44-ED on the vpp backend (internal/plugins/firewall/vpp/nat_linux.go)
  • on the nft backend the nf_conntrack TCP tracker follows every valid TCP sequence including simultaneous open, keeps an established connection for the Linux default of five days while the operator leaves its timeout unset, and keeps a connection across ICMP.
What the ledger says remains Endpoint-independent mapping is not guaranteed (RFC5382-4.1-1). An unsolicited inbound SYN is not held for six seconds: the NAT's own TCP stack answers it with a RST unless an operator filter drops it (RFC5382-4.3-1, RFC5382-4.3-2, RFC5382-4.3-3). Unless the operator sets them under system conntrack timeout tcp (written by addTCPTimeouts, accepting values down to 1 second), Linux's transitory TCP timeouts (10 to 120 seconds) stay below four minutes, and no value below four minutes is refused (RFC5382-5-2). The system conntrack timeout tcp established leaf accepts values down to one second, below the 2 hours 4 minutes the established idle-timeout may not go under (RFC5382-5-1). No hairpinning is configured (RFC5382-7.2-1). nf_nat checks collisions on the full reply tuple (nf_nat_used_tuple), so two internal endpoints can share one external port toward different remotes, the port overloading REQ-7 forbids (RFC5382-7.1-1). The TCP tracking, simultaneous-open and ICMP behaviors nf_conntrack performs on the nft backend are unverified on the vpp backend's VPP NAT44-ED path (RFC5382-4.2-1, RFC5382-4.2-2, RFC5382-7.3-1).
