Enrolment

Enrolled: Section 3 specifies the address order, length and shared-subnet conditions for the MP_REACH_NLRI next-hop field. Section 2 scope definitions and Section 4 BGP Identifier references are classified in rfc/extraction/rfc2545.json. Enrolled 2026-08-07.

What the public ledger says

Status: Partial

What the ledger says is covered Default/static, API-batch, queued-RIB and named-commit paths enforce next-hop address roles and final ordinary policy admission. Forwarding and stored replay judge the effective field after policy against one connected prefix containing the recipient and global next hop. Exact recipient-wire tests, distinct-endpoint topology, restored FRR captures and public SDK workflows cover supported own-address and received-pair cases. The earlier Supported claim counted tagged coverage, not proof of every condition.
What the ledger says remains Partial: the whole RFC2545-3-3 sentence remains unmet where only another router's global address is supplied on the common subnet. linkScope.linkLocalNextHop has no source for that router's Link-Local; the separate RFC2545-3-6 gap does not narrow the identical whole sentence. Configured speaker-owned Link-Local inclusion and the otherwise-global-only branch are implemented and exercised, including post-policy forwarding. No new Neighbor Discovery or liveness service is implied. Capability77 and VPN exceptions retain their separate draft/RFC4659 contracts.

RFC2545-3-1

A BGP speaker shall advertise to its peer in the Network Address of Next Hop field the global IPv6 address of the next hop, potentially followed by the link-local IPv6 address of the next hop. (§3)

Audit verdict: enforced (the tests do what the requirement demands), fresh. Rejudged 2026-10-23 by an independent auditor (not the author) after 010e649057, which makes the final writer (session_ownership.go adjOutWrite.allow) send a synthesized withdrawal only to a destination holding an adj-out record for the route. RFC 4271 Section 3.1 allows a withdrawal only for "the IP prefix that expresses the destination for a previously advertised route". RFC 2545 Section 3: "A BGP speaker shall advertise to its peer in the Network Address of Next Hop field the global IPv6 address of the next hop, potentially followed by the link-local IPv6 address of the next hop." The one moved unit is TestRFC2545ReceivedUnusableGlobalRefused (both tags): it now calls advertiseFirst, which plants the same route in the destination's adj-out through the raw session writer (ownershipWriterForward, bypassing the forward gates) and clears the socket, before the received UPDATE is forwarded. Every assertion is unchanged: exactly one recipient UPDATE; for usable globals (single and paired) an exact MP_REACH with the received next hop; for unspecified and multicast globals (single and paired, with and without capability 77) no MP_REACH and the exact MP_UNREACH; no legacy NLRI. Bypassing the unusable-global egress gate would send an announcement and fail; rejecting every IPv6 next hop would withdraw the valid cases and fail; so the negative (never announced) and positive (usable global advertised) both still discriminate. The planted earlier generation carries the invalid hop itself; that is fixture state that makes the withdrawal legitimate, not a claim about Ze's output. The changed helper rfc2545ReceiveForwardPublished primes adj-out only where it expects a gate withdrawal; its tagged callers are byte-identical and keep their prior judgement. Every other tagged carrier named by the 2026-10-22 verdict is byte-identical and its judgement stands (scoped origination wrappers, builder/forward admission, link-local append when Section 3 holds, SR Policy wire, encode/exabgp/plugin .ci). RFC2545-3-2

The value of the Length of Next Hop Network Address field on a MP_REACH_NLRI attribute shall be set to 16, when only a global address is present, or 32 if a link-local address is also included in the Next Hop field. (§3)

Audit verdict: enforced (the tests do what the requirement demands), fresh. Incremental M30 source audit, 2026-10-22; retain the prior full-audit verdict without claiming to reread untouched carriers. RFC 2545 Section 3: "The value of the Length of Next Hop Network Address field on a MP_REACH_NLRI attribute shall be set to 16, when only a global address is present, or 32 if a link-local address is also included in the Next Hop field." The four scoped origination tagged units still invoke the complete shared helper, whose literal successful frames pin next-hop length 0x10 and the exact address/NLRI bytes. Four-octet and VPN-shaped 24/48-octet IPv6-unicast policy fields remain negative cases, with exact refusal and no successful advertisement accounting, rather than shortened successful frames. Read message.ValidateMPNextHop and ValidateIPv6NextHop, plus writeUpdateGated, which judges actual effective field width and roles before ownership admission. Correct MP offerings on both sides prevent the family consumer from discarding valid IPv6; the mixed case explicitly offers IPv4 too, preserving its exact sibling oracle. The ordinary joint-subnet helpers independently retain exact complete 0x20 pair and 0x10 global-only frames on API batch, queued drain and named commit. The ordinary_growth_test.go extended-message override now appends ExtendedMessage to the dual-family offering rather than erasing it; its unchanged helper requires the complete 32-octet pair, bounded frames, every ordered NLRI exactly once, one policy invocation and a healthy subsequent send. This growth fixture is contextual evidence, not a new RFC tag. No assertion, claim or timeout changed in the scoped diff. Source-semantic retention only: no test, race run, discriminator, gate or stamp executed here.
