How to read the colors

A color names what the measure MEANS, not how well Ze scores on it. Green is a good outcome at any value, red is a bad one, and neither a population nor a scope count is an outcome, so both take no color. The number under the label is what says how far Ze has got.

RFC2545-3-1

A BGP speaker shall advertise to its peer in the Network Address of Next Hop field the global IPv6 address of the next hop, potentially followed by the link-local IPv6 address of the next hop. (§3)

Audit verdict: enforced (the tests do what the requirement demands), fresh. Re-judged 2026-10-31 by an independent auditor who authored none of 6b0a55d9b9. RFC 2545 Section 3 read: "A BGP speaker shall advertise to its peer in the Network Address of Next Hop field the global IPv6 address of the next hop, potentially followed by the link-local IPv6 address of the next hop." 6b0a55d9b9 changed only the RFC2545-3-1 claim comment of test/encode/new-v6.ci; every cmd, expect and config line and the 3-2 and 3-4 tags are byte-identical. The old claim (nothing appended after the global) was 3-4's subject; the new one says the field carries the configured next hop's own global and an encoder writing any other address in the first slot fails. That is exactly what the body checks: both expect=bgp lines pin the whole UPDATE, 800E26 0002 01 10 then 2A020B80000000020000000000000001, the configured 2A02:B80:0:2::1, so the claim no longer states more than the bytes prove. Producer read: buildMPReach (update_build.go) writes nextHops[0] = p.NextHop and appends unicastLinkLocalNextHop(p) only when valid; MPReachNLRI.WriteTo writes the list in order.
