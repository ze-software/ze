## Enrolment

Enrolled: Section 3 specifies the address order, length and shared-subnet conditions for the MP_REACH_NLRI next-hop field. Section 2 scope definitions and Section 4 BGP Identifier references are classified in rfc/extraction/rfc2545.json. Enrolled 2026-08-07.

## What the public ledger says

**Status:** Partial

**What the ledger says is covered**

- IPv6-unicast default/static, API-batch, queued-RIB and named-commit paths enforce next-hop address roles
- final ordinary export-policy admission also checks wire length and negotiated capability 77. Native tests assert recipient bytes and writer/transaction accounting. Live forwarding and stored replay preserve the complete address pair under the configured connected-prefix predicate. The earlier Supported claim counted tagged coverage as proof of every address and subnet condition
- that was not whole-clause evidence.


**What the ledger says remains**

Final verification and native rejudgment remain open. Finite-family regressions reproduce admission or dispatch defects in IPv6 multicast/labeled routes, RFC8950 IPv4 routes and SR Policy; their distinct native, mapped and RD-bearing field contracts must not be conflated. Effective forwarding-policy wrong-width and addressless-field repairs also await final combined proof. Connected-prefix checks do not establish independent adjacency of the remote next-hop entity, so the Section 3 shared-subnet clauses remain only partly proven.

- **The RFC2545-3-6 gap also remains:** `linkScope.linkLocalNextHop` has no source for another router's link-local address and sends its global address alone. Capability code 77 is draft-based, not an RFC2545 capability; VPN next-hop exceptions have their own RFC4659 conditions.
