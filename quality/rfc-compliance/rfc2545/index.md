## Enrolment

Enrolled: Section 3 specifies the address order, length and shared-subnet conditions for the MP_REACH_NLRI next-hop field. Section 2 scope definitions and Section 4 BGP Identifier references are classified in rfc/extraction/rfc2545.json. Enrolled 2026-08-07.

## What the public ledger says

**Status:** Partial

**What the ledger says is covered**

Default/static, API-batch, queued-RIB and named-commit paths enforce next-hop address roles and final ordinary policy admission. Forwarding and stored replay judge the effective field after policy against one connected prefix containing the recipient and global next hop. Exact recipient-wire tests, distinct-endpoint topology, restored FRR captures and public SDK workflows cover supported own-address and received-pair cases. The earlier Supported claim counted tagged coverage, not proof of every condition.

**What the ledger says remains**

Partial: the whole [`RFC2545-3-3`](#rfc2545-3-3) sentence remains unmet where only another router's global address is supplied on the common subnet. `linkScope.linkLocalNextHop` has no source for that router's Link-Local; the separate [`RFC2545-3-6`](#rfc2545-3-6) gap does not narrow the identical whole sentence. Configured speaker-owned Link-Local inclusion and the otherwise-global-only branch are implemented and exercised, including post-policy forwarding. No new Neighbor Discovery or liveness service is implied. Capability77 and VPN exceptions retain their separate draft/RFC4659 contracts.
