## What the AGPLv3 gives you

You are free to run Ze for any purpose, study how it works, modify it, and
share it, including modified versions. Those freedoms come with the usual
copyleft condition: anything you distribute that is derived from Ze must
itself be offered under the AGPLv3, with its source available.

## The network clause

The AGPL adds one obligation on top of the GPL. If you run a **modified** Ze
and let other people use it over a network, you have to make your modified
source available to those users. Running an unmodified Ze to route your own
traffic does not trigger this: the clause is about offering a changed version
as a service to others.
