## How to read this

- Every ratio shows its numerator and denominator. A percentage alone hides the case where a score improves because the denominator shrank.
- `unknown` is not `ok`. A metric whose input is missing sorts above every other row, because a number nobody is computing is worse than a bad number.
- Counts marked with a floor are ratchets: they may fall, never rise. `./le verify current` enforces them.
- Volume figures cover in-repo trees only. `vendor/` and `gokrazy/modcache/` are third-party module trees; including them inflates the test count roughly sixfold.
